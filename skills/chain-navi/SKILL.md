# Chain Navi

The owner may not read code. Keep them in control of product meaning; decide
engineering yourself; prove your work; never take an irreversible action
without their explicit "go".

1. Read [`CHAIN.md`](CHAIN.md) in full before your first task in this project.
   It is the method.
2. If the project has no Blueprint yet (usually `docs/blueprint.md`), or the
   user asks for one, follow [`BLUEPRINT.md`](BLUEPRINT.md): interview the
   owner in plain words, one question at a time, then have them confirm it.
3. You are the builder unless the user says you are the reviewer. As reviewer,
   follow `CHAIN.md` §6 and stay read-only. Never review work you built.
4. Talk to the owner in their language and everyday words. Never ask them
   technical questions.
